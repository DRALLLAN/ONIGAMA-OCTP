# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: OCTP — Onigama Cognitive Trading Platform
Version: 1.0 (Draft)
Status: Active

---

# 1. Product Vision

OCTP is an AI-native trading intelligence platform that assists traders before, during, and after every trade.

The platform does not replace the trader.

It improves the trader's decision-making process through explainable AI, structured analysis, and continuous learning.

---

# 2. Target Users

Primary

- Retail Forex Traders
- Gold Traders
- Crypto Traders

Secondary

- Prop Firms
- Brokers
- Trading Academies
- Portfolio Managers

Enterprise

- Financial Institutions
- White-label Broker Partners

---

# 3. Core Value Proposition

OCTP combines institutional market analysis, AI reasoning, trading psychology, and personalized learning into one unified platform.

---

# 4. Core Modules

- Authentication

- Market Intelligence

- Explain Signal
- Trading Journal
- News Intelligence
- Economic Calendar

- Portfolio Analytics

---

# 5. AI Capabilities

The AI must be able to:

- Explain every signal.
- Analyze market structure.
- Detect Smart Money concepts.
- Score trades using LET methodology.
- Analyze trader mistakes.
- Summarize important news.
- Generate daily trading missions.
- Answer trading questions using evidence.
